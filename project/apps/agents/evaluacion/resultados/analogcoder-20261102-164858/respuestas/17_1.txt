## Task 1: Design Plan

A Wien bridge oscillator uses a non-inverting amplifier with a frequency-selective RC bridge in its positive feedback path and an amplitude-setting resistor network in its negative feedback path.

**Op-amp core (modeled as a high-gain VCVS):**
- Voltage-controlled voltage source `EOP`, gain A = 1e5.
- Non-inverting input: `Vplus`. Inverting input: the "−" node, implemented as `Vminus` because "-" is not a valid SPICE node identifier.
- Output: `Vout`.

**Positive feedback (frequency-selecting Wien bridge):**
- Series arm from `Vout` to `Vplus`: R1 = 10 kΩ (`Vout`→`N1`), C1 = 10 nF (`N1`→`Vplus`).
- Shunt arm from `Vplus` to ground: R2 = 10 kΩ, C2 = 10 nF (both `Vplus`→ground).
- Oscillation frequency: f₀ = 1/(2πRC) = 1/(2π · 10 kΩ · 10 nF) ≈ 1.59 kHz.
- At f₀, the bridge gives a feedback fraction of 1/3 with zero phase shift.

**Negative feedback (amplitude control):**
- R3 = 21 kΩ from `Vout` to `Vminus`. This is slightly above 2·R4 so the loop gain exceeds 3 at start-up.
- R4 = 10 kΩ from `Vminus` to ground. Ideal non-inverting gain = 1 + R3/R4 ≈ 3.1, which is just above the required 3.
- Two anti-parallel diodes (D1, D2, model `DMOD`, Is = 1e-14) are placed across R3. At small amplitude they are off and the gain is set by R3. At large amplitude they conduct, lower the gain toward 3, and clamp the output to a stable amplitude of roughly 1 V.

**Node summary:** `Vout`, `N1`, `Vplus`, `Vminus`, ground (`0`).

## Task 2: PySpice Code

```python
from PySpice.Spice.Netlist import Circuit
from PySpice.Unit import *

circuit = Circuit('Wien Bridge Oscillator')

# Diode model for amplitude stabilization
circuit.model('DMOD', 'D', Is=1e-14, N=1.0)

# Op-amp modeled as a high-gain voltage-controlled voltage source
# VCVS(name, out_plus, out_minus, in_plus, in_minus, gain)
circuit.VCVS('OP', 'Vout', circuit.gnd, 'Vplus', 'Vminus', 1e5)

# Positive feedback: Wien bridge (frequency-selective network)
# Series RC arm: Vout -> N1 -> Vplus
circuit.R('1', 'Vout', 'N1', 10@u_kΩ)
circuit.C('1', 'N1', 'Vplus', 10@u_nF)
# Shunt RC arm: Vplus -> ground
circuit.R('2', 'Vplus', circuit.gnd, 10@u_kΩ)
circuit.C('2', 'Vplus', circuit.gnd, 10@u_nF)

# Negative feedback: gain setting network with amplitude limiting diodes
circuit.R('3', 'Vout', 'Vminus', 21@u_kΩ)
circuit.R('4', 'Vminus', circuit.gnd, 10@u_kΩ)
# Anti-parallel diodes across R3 for amplitude stabilization
circuit.D('1', 'Vout', 'Vminus', model='DMOD')
circuit.D('2', 'Vminus', 'Vout', model='DMOD')

# Analysis Part
simulator = circuit.simulator()
```
