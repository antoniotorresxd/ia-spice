* Schmitt trigger no inversor con realimentacion positiva
* Valores comerciales (circuito): ideal -> comercial
*   Rin: 39kohm -> 39kohm [E24, +0.00%]
*   Rf: 100kohm -> 100kohm [E24, +0.00%]
VDD VDD 0 DC 5
VREF VREF 0 DC 2.5
Vstim Vin 0 PULSE(0 5 1m 1m 1m 10m 20m)
Rin Vin vp 39k
Rf Vout vp 100k
Bcmp Vout 0 V=V(VDD)*(1+tanh(1e4*(V(vp)-V(VREF))))/2
.control
tran 1u 40m
meas tran v_high_threshold FIND v(Vin) WHEN v(Vout)=2.5 RISE=1
echo $&v_high_threshold > output.txt
.endc
.end
