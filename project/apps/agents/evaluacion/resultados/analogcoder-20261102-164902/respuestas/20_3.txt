### Task 1

#### Design overview

Use an inverting summing network driven by a two-stage CMOS op amp. The op amp’s non-inverting input is grounded, and its inverting input is the summing node `Vsum`.

A negative output requires a negative supply rail. The circuit therefore uses `Vdd = +5 V` and an additional `Vss = -5 V`; with only a +5 V supply and ground, the op amp could not produce the required negative output for positive inputs.

#### Summing network

- `Rin1` connects `Vin1` to `Vsum`.
- `Rin2` connects `Vin2` to `Vsum`.
- `Rf` connects `Vout` to `Vsum`.
- Set all three resistors to 10 kΩ. With an ideal op amp, this gives  
  \[
  Vout = -(Vin1 + Vin2).
  \]

#### CMOS op amp

- **M1, M2 — PMOS differential input pair:** Their gates connect to `Vsum` and ground, respectively. Their sources share `Ntail`.
- **M3 — PMOS tail-current source:** Its source and bulk connect to `Vdd`; its gate connects to `VBP`; its drain connects to `Ntail`.
- **M4, M5 — NMOS current-mirror load:** M4 is diode-connected at `N1`; M5’s gate connects to `N1`. Their sources and bulks connect to `Vss`. The drains connect to `N1` and `N2`, respectively.
- **M6 — NMOS second-stage common-source device:** Its gate connects to `N2`; its source and bulk connect to `Vss`; its drain connects to `Vout`.
- **M7 — PMOS second-stage current-source load:** Its source and bulk connect to `Vdd`; its gate connects to `VBP`; its drain connects to `Vout`.
- **Bias:** `VBP = 4.2 V` biases the PMOS current sources. The example input sources are set to 0.1 V DC each, so the intended output is approximately −0.2 V.

All MOSFETs use a 2 µm channel length and nominal widths. The model is a simple Level 1 model, so practical gain, offset, and output swing will differ from an ideal op amp.

### Task 2

```python
from PySpice.Spice.Netlist import Circuit

circuit = Circuit('CMOS Opamp Inverting Adder')

# Simple Level 1 MOSFET models
circuit.model('nmos_model', 'nmos', level=1, kp=100e-6, vto=0.5)
circuit.model('pmos_model', 'pmos', level=1, kp=50e-6, vto=-0.5)

# Supplies: +5 V and -5 V are used so Vout can be negative
circuit.V('dd', 'Vdd', circuit.gnd, 5.0)
circuit.V('ss', 'Vss', circuit.gnd, -5.0)

# DC input examples and PMOS current-source bias
circuit.V('in1', 'Vin1', circuit.gnd, 0.1)
circuit.V('in2', 'Vin2', circuit.gnd, 0.1)
circuit.V('bp', 'VBP', circuit.gnd, 4.2)

# Inverting summing network
circuit.R('in1', 'Vin1', 'Vsum', 10e3)
circuit.R('in2', 'Vin2', 'Vsum', 10e3)
circuit.R('f', 'Vout', 'Vsum', 10e3)

# First stage: PMOS differential pair with NMOS current-mirror load
# MOSFET arguments: name, drain, gate, source, bulk, model, w, l
circuit.MOSFET('1', 'N1', 'Vsum', 'Ntail', 'Ntail',
               model='pmos_model', w=100e-6, l=2e-6)
circuit.MOSFET('2', 'N2', circuit.gnd, 'Ntail', 'Ntail',
               model='pmos_model', w=100e-6, l=2e-6)
circuit.MOSFET('3', 'Ntail', 'VBP', 'Vdd', 'Vdd',
               model='pmos_model', w=100e-6, l=2e-6)

circuit.MOSFET('4', 'N1', 'N1', 'Vss', 'Vss',
               model='nmos_model', w=50e-6, l=2e-6)
circuit.MOSFET('5', 'N2', 'N1', 'Vss', 'Vss',
               model='nmos_model', w=50e-6, l=2e-6)

# Second stage: NMOS common-source amplifier with PMOS current-source load
circuit.MOSFET('6', 'Vout', 'N2', 'Vss', 'Vss',
               model='nmos_model', w=50e-6, l=2e-6)
circuit.MOSFET('7', 'Vout', 'VBP', 'Vdd', 'Vdd',
               model='pmos_model', w=50e-6, l=2e-6)

simulator = circuit.simulator()
```
