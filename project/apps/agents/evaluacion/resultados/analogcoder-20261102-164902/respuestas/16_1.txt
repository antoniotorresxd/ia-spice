## Task 1

### Components and topology

- **M1**: NMOS common-source amplifier transistor.
- **RD**: Drain load resistor, connected from **Vdd** to **Vout**.
- **R1–R3**: Three series resistors forming the RC feedback ladder.
- **C1–C3**: Shunt capacitors from the ladder nodes to ground.
- **C4**: Large coupling capacitor that passes the feedback signal to the gate while isolating the gate’s DC bias.
- **RB1, RB2**: Gate-bias divider.
- **Vdd**: 5 V supply.

### Connections

1. **Amplifier**
   - M1 drain connects to **Vout**.
   - M1 source and bulk connect to ground.
   - RD connects from **Vdd** to **Vout**.
   - The common-source stage inverts the signal by approximately 180°.

2. **RC phase-shift feedback network**
   - Connect **Vout → R1 → N1 → R2 → N2 → R3 → N3**.
   - Connect C1 from **N1** to ground, C2 from **N2** to ground, and C3 from **N3** to ground.
   - Connect C4 from **N3** to the gate-bias node **Vg**. C4 is large so it contributes little additional phase shift at the oscillation frequency while blocking DC.

3. **Gate bias**
   - RB1 connects from **Vdd** to **Vg**.
   - RB2 connects from **Vg** to ground.
   - The divider sets the gate voltage to about 0.63 V, slightly above the NMOS threshold.

The three-section RC network provides approximately 180° of phase shift and the common-source amplifier provides another 180°. With equal R and C values, the nominal oscillation frequency is approximately \(1/(2\pi RC\sqrt{6})\), or 6.5 kHz for the values below. The amplifier is sized to provide loop gain above the idealized minimum of about 29.

## Task 2

```python
from PySpice.Spice.Netlist import Circuit

circuit = Circuit('RC Phase-Shift Oscillator')

# NMOS model
circuit.model('nmos_model', 'nmos', level=1, kp=100e-6, vto=0.5)

# 5 V supply
circuit.V('dd', 'Vdd', circuit.gnd, 5.0)

# Common-source amplifier and drain load
circuit.MOSFET(
    '1', 'Vout', 'Vg', circuit.gnd, circuit.gnd,
    model='nmos_model', w=500e-6, l=1e-6
)
circuit.R('D', 'Vdd', 'Vout', 6.8e3)

# Three-section RC phase-shift network
circuit.R('1', 'Vout', 'N1', 10e3)
circuit.R('2', 'N1', 'N2', 10e3)
circuit.R('3', 'N2', 'N3', 10e3)

circuit.C('1', 'N1', circuit.gnd, 10e-9)
circuit.C('2', 'N2', circuit.gnd, 10e-9)
circuit.C('3', 'N3', circuit.gnd, 10e-9)

# AC feedback coupling to the biased amplifier gate
circuit.C('4', 'N3', 'Vg', 1e-6)

# Gate-bias divider; Vg is approximately 0.63 V
circuit.R('B1', 'Vdd', 'Vg', 4.7e6)
circuit.R('B2', 'Vg', circuit.gnd, 680e3)

simulator = circuit.simulator()
```
