### Task 1 — Design Plan

This Wien Bridge oscillator uses a high-gain voltage-controlled voltage source (VCVS) as an idealized op-amp, with a single 5 V supply and a 2.5 V reference. The output oscillates around **Vref = 2.5 V**; there is no external input signal.

#### Devices and interconnections

- **Vdd supply:** 5 V from `Vdd` to ground.
- **Reference source:** 2.5 V from `Vref` to ground. This biases the oscillator’s signal path to mid-supply.
- **A1, op-amp model:** A finite-gain VCVS with output terminals `Vout` and `Vref`, and control inputs `Wp` and `Wm`. The non-inverting input is `Wp`; the inverting input is `Wm`.
- **Wien bridge network:**
  - `Cw1` connects `Vout` to `Nw`.
  - `Rw1` connects `Nw` to `Wp`.
  - `Rw2` and `Cw2` are in parallel between `Wp` and `Vref`.
  - With both resistors at 10 kΩ and both capacitors at 10 nF, the nominal oscillation frequency is approximately **1.59 kHz**.
- **Gain and amplitude stabilization:**
  - `Rfb1` and `Rfb2` form the feedback path from `Vout` to `Wm`.
  - `Rg` connects `Wm` to `Vref`.
  - At small signal, the feedback resistor total is 22 kΩ and `Rg` is 10 kΩ, giving a non-inverting gain of 3.2—slightly above the Wien bridge’s oscillation requirement of 3 to encourage startup.
  - Antiparallel diodes across `Rfb2` conduct as the output grows, reducing the effective feedback resistance and limiting the amplitude.

The VCVS is a compact behavioral op-amp model, rather than a transistor-level op-amp. In a transient simulation, a small initial disturbance or circuit noise is needed to start oscillation from an exactly balanced state.

### Task 2 — PySpice Code

```python
from PySpice.Spice.Netlist import Circuit

circuit = Circuit('Wien Bridge Oscillator')

# Supply and mid-supply reference
circuit.V('dd', 'Vdd', circuit.gnd, 5.0)
circuit.V('ref', 'Vref', circuit.gnd, 2.5)

# Diode model for amplitude stabilization
circuit.model('dlim', 'D', is_=1e-14, n=1.0)

# Wien bridge: series RC from output to non-inverting input,
# parallel RC from non-inverting input to the reference
circuit.C('w1', 'Vout', 'Nw', 10e-9)
circuit.R('w1', 'Nw', 'Wp', 10e3)
circuit.R('w2', 'Wp', 'Vref', 10e3)
circuit.C('w2', 'Wp', 'Vref', 10e-9)

# Op-amp represented by a high-gain VCVS.
# Its output is referenced to Vref, centering the signal at mid-supply.
circuit.VCVS('A1', 'Vout', 'Vref', 'Wp', 'Wm', voltage_gain=1e5)

# Non-inverting amplifier feedback network
circuit.R('fb1', 'Vout', 'Nfb', 10e3)
circuit.R('fb2', 'Nfb', 'Wm', 12e3)
circuit.R('g', 'Wm', 'Vref', 10e3)

# Antiparallel limiter diodes across Rfb2
circuit.D('lim1', 'Nfb', 'Wm', model='dlim')
circuit.D('lim2', 'Wm', 'Nfb', model='dlim')

simulator = circuit.simulator()
```
