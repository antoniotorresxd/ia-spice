### Task 1 — Design plan

This is a single-frequency Wien Bridge oscillator. It has no external input signal; the **Vout** signal is fed back through a frequency-selective RC bridge. The amplifier is represented in SPICE by an ideal voltage-controlled voltage source (VCVS), a convenient behavioral model for an op-amp.

#### Components and interconnections

- **Supply and midpoint reference**
  - `Vdd`: 5 V supply.
  - `R3` and `R4`: equal 100 kΩ resistors forming a 2.5 V midpoint reference, `Vmid`.
  - `C3`: bypasses `Vmid` to ground to make it a low-impedance AC reference.

- **Wien Bridge feedback network**
  - `C1` and `R1`: connected in series from `Vout` to `Wien`.
  - `R2` and `C2`: connected in parallel from `Wien` to `Vmid`.
  - With equal 10 kΩ resistors and equal 10 nF capacitors, the nominal oscillation frequency is
    \[
    f_0=\frac{1}{2\pi RC}\approx 1.59\ \text{kHz}.
    \]
  - At this frequency, the bridge has zero phase shift and an attenuation of approximately one third.

- **Amplifier and amplitude limiting**
  - `EA`: VCVS amplifier with gain 3.1, slightly greater than the required gain of 3 for startup.
  - Its input is the difference between `Wien` and `Vmid`; a 2.5 V offset shifts the amplifier output to the single-supply midpoint.
  - `R5` provides output isolation. `D1` and `D2` clamp `Vout` near the supply rails, limiting the oscillation amplitude.

Real circuits start from noise or a small disturbance. A deterministic transient simulation may require an initial perturbation to show startup; the code below defines the circuit and simulator without running an analysis.

### Task 2 — PySpice code

```python
from PySpice.Spice.Netlist import Circuit
from PySpice.Unit import *

circuit = Circuit('Wien Bridge Oscillator')

# 5 V supply and AC-grounded midpoint reference
circuit.V('dd', 'Vdd', circuit.gnd, 5 @ u_V)
circuit.R('3', 'Vdd', 'Vmid', 100 @ u_kΩ)
circuit.R('4', 'Vmid', circuit.gnd, 100 @ u_kΩ)
circuit.C('3', 'Vmid', circuit.gnd, 10 @ u_uF)

# Wien bridge: series RC from output to Wien node,
# and parallel RC from Wien node to the midpoint reference
circuit.C('1', 'Vout', 'Nseries', 10 @ u_nF)
circuit.R('1', 'Nseries', 'Wien', 10 @ u_kΩ)
circuit.R('2', 'Wien', 'Vmid', 10 @ u_kΩ)
circuit.C('2', 'Wien', 'Vmid', 10 @ u_nF)

# Behavioral non-inverting amplifier: gain slightly above 3 for startup.
# The VCVS output is referenced to Vmid by adding a 2.5 V DC offset.
circuit.VCVS('A', 'Araw', circuit.gnd, 'Wien', 'Vmid', 3.1)
circuit.V('offset', 'Vdrive', 'Araw', 2.5 @ u_V)
circuit.R('5', 'Vdrive', 'Vout', 100 @ u_Ω)

# Output amplitude clamps to the supply rails
circuit.model('clamp_d', 'D', IS=1e-14, N=1)
circuit.D('1', circuit.gnd, 'Vout', model='clamp_d')
circuit.D('2', 'Vout', 'Vdd', model='clamp_d')

simulator = circuit.simulator()
```
