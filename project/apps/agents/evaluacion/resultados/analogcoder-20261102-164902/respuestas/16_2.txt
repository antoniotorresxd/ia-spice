## Task 1 — Design plan

Use an **inverting common-source amplifier** and a three-section RC feedback ladder. The common-source stage contributes 180° of phase inversion; the ladder contributes approximately another 180° at its oscillation frequency, giving positive feedback.

### Devices and connections

- **M1 — NMOS amplifier**
  - Drain: `Vout`
  - Gate: `Vg`
  - Source and bulk: ground
  - Nominal sizing: \(W=80\,\mu\text{m}\), \(L=1\,\mu\text{m}\)
- **R_D — Drain load**
  - Connect `Vdd` to `Vout`
  - Value: 100 kΩ
- **Three-section RC feedback ladder**
  - `Vout` → C1 → `N1` → C2 → `N2` → C3 → `Vg`
  - Connect a 1 MΩ resistor from each of `N1`, `N2`, and `Vg` to ground.
  - Use 1 nF for each capacitor.
  - With equal nominal sections, the unloaded ladder’s phase-shift frequency is approximately \(1/(2\pi RC\sqrt{6})\), or about 65 Hz.
- **Gate bias**
  - Connect 6.8 MΩ from `Vdd` to `Vg`, and 10 MΩ from `Vg` to ground.
  - The final ladder resistor also connects `Vg` to ground, so it is included when setting the gate bias. These values place `Vg` near 0.59 V, slightly above the assumed 0.5 V NMOS threshold.

The amplifier is biased by the resistor network; the three RC sections provide frequency-selective positive feedback. In a real transient simulation, small startup noise or an initial disturbance may be needed to initiate oscillation.

## Task 2 — PySpice code

```python
from PySpice.Spice.Netlist import Circuit

circuit = Circuit('RC Phase-Shift Oscillator')

# NMOS model and 5 V supply
circuit.model('nmos_model', 'nmos', level=1, kp=100e-6, vto=0.5)
circuit.V('dd', 'Vdd', circuit.gnd, 5.0)

# Common-source amplifier
circuit.MOSFET(
    '1', 'Vout', 'Vg', circuit.gnd, circuit.gnd,
    model='nmos_model', w=80e-6, l=1e-6
)
circuit.R('D', 'Vdd', 'Vout', 100e3)

# Three-section RC phase-shift feedback ladder
circuit.C('1', 'Vout', 'N1', 1e-9)
circuit.R('1', 'N1', circuit.gnd, 1e6)

circuit.C('2', 'N1', 'N2', 1e-9)
circuit.R('2', 'N2', circuit.gnd, 1e6)

circuit.C('3', 'N2', 'Vg', 1e-9)
circuit.R('3', 'Vg', circuit.gnd, 1e6)

# Gate-bias network; the ladder's final resistor also loads Vg at DC
circuit.R('bias_top', 'Vdd', 'Vg', 6.8e6)
circuit.R('bias_bottom', 'Vg', circuit.gnd, 10e6)

simulator = circuit.simulator()
```
