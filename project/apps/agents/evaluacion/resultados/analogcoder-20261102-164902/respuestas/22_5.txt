### Task 1 — Design plan

Use an op-amp comparator with positive feedback to its non-inverting input. This gives a **non-inverting Schmitt trigger**: as `Vin` rises through the upper threshold, `Vout` switches high; as `Vin` falls through the lower threshold, `Vout` switches low.

#### Devices and connections

- **Supply:** `Vdd = 5 V`, with ground as the negative rail.
- **Reference divider:** two 10 kΩ resistors from `Vdd` to ground create `Vref ≈ 2.5 V`.
- **Op-amp input nodes:**
  - Non-inverting input: `Vplus`.
  - Inverting input: `Vminus`, connected to `Vref`.
- **Input resistor:** `Rin = 100 kΩ` from `Vin` to `Vplus`.
- **Positive-feedback resistor:** `Rf = 470 kΩ` from `Vout` to `Vplus`.
- **Op-amp model:** a high-gain voltage-controlled voltage source, with a series output resistor and diode clamps to approximate the 0–5 V output range.
- **Input source:** a 3.5 V DC source at `Vin`, chosen to place the circuit in its high-output state for the operating-point calculation.

With ideal output levels of 0 V and 5 V, the approximate switching thresholds are 3.03 V on a rising input and 1.97 V on a falling input. The diode clamps make the actual levels and thresholds approximate.

### Task 2 — PySpice code

```python
from PySpice.Spice.Netlist import Circuit
from PySpice.Unit import *

circuit = Circuit('Non-Inverting Schmitt Trigger')

# Supply and DC input
circuit.V('dd', 'Vdd', circuit.gnd, 5.0)
circuit.V('in', 'Vin', circuit.gnd, 3.5)

# 2.5 V reference divider
circuit.R('ref_top', 'Vdd', 'Vref', 10@u_kΩ)
circuit.R('ref_bottom', 'Vref', circuit.gnd, 10@u_kΩ)

# Non-inverting Schmitt-trigger input and positive feedback
circuit.R('in', 'Vin', 'Vplus', 100@u_kΩ)
circuit.R('fb', 'Vout', 'Vplus', 470@u_kΩ)

# Idealized high-gain op-amp stage
circuit.VCVS(
    'Aop',
    'Vraw',
    circuit.gnd,
    'Vplus',
    'Vref',
    voltage_gain=1e5
)

# Output isolation and rail clamps
circuit.R('out', 'Vraw', 'Vout', 100@u_Ω)
circuit.model('Dclamp', 'D', is_=1e-14, n=1)
circuit.D('clamp_low', circuit.gnd, 'Vout', model='Dclamp')
circuit.D('clamp_high', 'Vout', 'Vdd', model='Dclamp')

simulator = circuit.simulator()
```
